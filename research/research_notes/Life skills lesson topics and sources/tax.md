# Tax: practical knowledge for SA, UK and US life-skills courses (as of 4 Nov 2026)

Scope: (a) gaps in the existing 6-lesson South African course; (b) prioritised UK and US lesson lists; (c) survey evidence; (d) source licence/reuse terms. Tax years: SA 2027 year of assessment (1 Mar 2026 – 28 Feb 2027); UK 2026/27 (6 Apr 2026 – 5 Apr 2027); US tax year 2026 (filed in 2027).

Note on currency: the UK held an Autumn Budget on 28 Oct 2026 (Chancellor John Healey). Only its ISA change was verified here (see UK section). Other UK figures below come from gov.uk pages for 2026/27 and are in force for this tax year, but any announced changes for 2027/28 were not reviewed.

## (a) South Africa: gaps worth adding as lessons

### Takeaway
SARS's own Budget 2026 FAQ covers almost every proposed gap topic with fresh 2026/27 figures, and several of them changed a lot in Budget 2026 (TFSA limit up to R46,000, CGT exclusions up, VAT and turnover tax thresholds up to R2.3m, retirement deduction cap R430,000). Those changes are good reasons to add lessons. Recommended additions, in priority order: (1) Getting a tax number for a first job; (2) Interest, dividends and the TFSA; (3) Two-pot withdrawals and tax directives; (4) Side hustles, gig work and crypto; (5) Auto-assessment, owing SARS and debt relief; (6) Capital gains tax basics; (7) Working abroad and tax residency; (8) VAT and turnover tax for micro-businesses; (9) Deceased estates basics.

### Cited Findings
**Base figures (for refreshing lesson 1 and lesson 4)**
- The 2026/27 brackets were raised 3.4% for inflation, the first inflation relief since 2023/24. They are 18% up to R245,100; 26% to R383,100; 31% to R530,200; 36% to R695,800; 39% to R887,000; 41% to R1,878,600; and 45% above that. — [SARS Budget 2026 FAQ](https://www.sars.gov.za/about/sars-tax-and-customs-system/budget/budget-2026-frequently-asked-questions/)
- Rebates are R17,820 (primary), R9,765 (secondary, 65+) and R3,249 (tertiary, 75+). The tax-free thresholds are R99,000 under 65, R153,250 for ages 65–74 and R171,300 for 75+. — [SARS Budget 2026 FAQ](https://www.sars.gov.za/about/sars-tax-and-customs-system/budget/budget-2026-frequently-asked-questions/)
- The medical scheme fees tax credit is R376/month for each of the first two people covered and R254/month for each additional dependant. — [SARS Budget 2026 FAQ](https://www.sars.gov.za/about/sars-tax-and-customs-system/budget/budget-2026-frequently-asked-questions/)
- Retirement fund contributions are deductible up to 27.5% of the greater of remuneration or taxable income, capped at R430,000 (up from R350,000, the first change since 2016). Any excess carries forward. — [SARS Budget 2026 FAQ](https://www.sars.gov.za/about/sars-tax-and-customs-system/budget/budget-2026-frequently-asked-questions/)

**Tax number registration for first jobbers**
- From the 202602 reconciliation period, a valid Income Tax Reference Number on IRP5/IT3(a) certificates is mandatory for employees who must register. eFiling will reject an employer's EMP501 if any such employee lacks one. Employers register employees via ITREG or BundleReg on eFiling, or at a branch by appointment. — [SARS Budget 2026 FAQ](https://www.sars.gov.za/about/sars-tax-and-customs-system/budget/budget-2026-frequently-asked-questions/)
- The national minimum wage is R30.23/hour from 2 March 2026. UIF is 1% from the employee plus 1% from the employer. — [SARS Budget 2026 FAQ](https://www.sars.gov.za/about/sars-tax-and-customs-system/budget/budget-2026-frequently-asked-questions/)

**Interest, dividends and TFSAs**
- SA-source interest is exempt up to R23,800/year under 65 and R34,500 for 65+. Interest earned inside a TFSA does not count towards these limits. — [SARS Budget 2026 FAQ](https://www.sars.gov.za/about/sars-tax-and-customs-system/budget/budget-2026-frequently-asked-questions/)
- The TFSA annual limit rose to R46,000 from 1 March 2026 (from R36,000). The lifetime limit stays at R500,000. Growth and withdrawals are tax-free. There is a 40% penalty on contributions above either limit, and the limits apply across all of a person's TFSAs combined. — [SARS Budget 2026 FAQ](https://www.sars.gov.za/about/sars-tax-and-customs-system/budget/budget-2026-frequently-asked-questions/); [SARS Tax Free Investments](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/personal-income-tax/tax-free-investments/); [2026 Budget Speech](https://archive.treasury.gov.za/documents/national%20budget/2026/speech/speech.pdf)
- Minors may hold TFSAs. Several TFSAs are allowed, but the annual limit is shared across all of them. — [SARS Tax Free Investments](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/personal-income-tax/tax-free-investments/)
- Dividends tax for non-residents is 20%. — [SARS Budget 2026 FAQ](https://www.sars.gov.za/about/sars-tax-and-customs-system/budget/budget-2026-frequently-asked-questions/)

**Two-pot withdrawals and tax directives**
- Savings-component (two-pot) withdrawals are taxed at the member's marginal rate, not under the lump-sum tables. The fund applies a SARS tax directive based on an estimated rate. If too little was withheld, the shortfall shows up on the auto-assessment (issued around 21 July 2026) and was due by 20 October 2026, with interest after that. The IRP5 source code is 3926. — [SARS Budget 2026 FAQ](https://www.sars.gov.za/about/sars-tax-and-customs-system/budget/budget-2026-frequently-asked-questions/)
- Withdrawal lump-sum table (resignation etc.): R0–R27,500 at 0%, rising to 36% above R1,089,000. Retirement lump sums: the first R550,000 is tax-free. Lump sums since 1 Oct 2007 are added together cumulatively, and the tables are unchanged from 2024/25 to 2026/27. — [SARS Budget 2026 FAQ](https://www.sars.gov.za/about/sars-tax-and-customs-system/budget/budget-2026-frequently-asked-questions/)
- SARS runs a dedicated tax-directives section, including one for income earned both locally and abroad. — [SARS tax directives archive](https://www.sars.gov.za/individuals/i-need-help-with-my-tax/your-tax-questions-answered/i-want-to-get-a-tax-directive/tax-directives-archive/)

**Side hustles, gig work, provisional tax and crypto**
- Anyone earning non-remuneration income (business, rental, investment) is a provisional taxpayer. They are exempt only if they run no business and either earn below R99,000, or earn only interest/dividends/rental/foreign income of R30,000 or less. The penalty is 20% if the second estimate is under 80% of final taxable income. — [SARS Budget 2026 FAQ](https://www.sars.gov.za/about/sars-tax-and-customs-system/budget/budget-2026-frequently-asked-questions/)
- Crypto traded frequently or in a business-like way is taxed as ordinary income. Crypto held as a long-term investment is subject to CGT (maximum effective rate 18%). The ITR12 source code for crypto trading is 4522. CARF crypto reporting by exchanges to SARS started on 2 March 2026. The Voluntary Disclosure Programme (VDP) is open only before SARS starts an audit. — [SARS Budget 2026 FAQ](https://www.sars.gov.za/about/sars-tax-and-customs-system/budget/budget-2026-frequently-asked-questions/)

**Auto-assessment, debt and enforcement**
- SARS can issue a Third-Party Appointment (AA88) telling a bank or employer to pay a taxpayer's debt directly to SARS, with no court order needed. The options for people who owe are a payment arrangement, a compromise, suspension pending objection, or the VDP. The interest rate on late payment is 10.25% from 2 March 2026. — [SARS Budget 2026 FAQ](https://www.sars.gov.za/about/sars-tax-and-customs-system/budget/budget-2026-frequently-asked-questions/)

**Capital gains tax**
- Maximum effective CGT is 18% for individuals. From 2 March 2026 the annual exclusion is R50,000 (was R40,000), the primary residence exclusion R3m (was R2m) and the death exclusion R440,000. For property, CGT is triggered when the sale agreement is signed, not when transfer is registered. — [SARS Budget 2026 FAQ](https://www.sars.gov.za/about/sars-tax-and-customs-system/budget/budget-2026-frequently-asked-questions/)

**VAT and turnover tax (micro-business)**
- From 1 April 2026, compulsory VAT registration applies above R2.3m of taxable supplies a year (was R1m), and voluntary registration above R120,000 (was R50,000). — [SARS Budget 2026 FAQ](https://www.sars.gov.za/about/sars-tax-and-customs-system/budget/budget-2026-frequently-asked-questions/)
- Turnover tax is optional and replaces income tax, VAT, provisional tax, CGT and dividends tax for businesses with turnover of R2.3m or less (was R1m). Rates are 0% up to R600,000, then 1%, 2% and 3% bands. It is not available where more than 20% of receipts come from professional services or investment income. Registration is via the SARS Online Query System, within 2 months of starting trading. — [SARS Budget 2026 FAQ](https://www.sars.gov.za/about/sars-tax-and-customs-system/budget/budget-2026-frequently-asked-questions/)

**Working abroad**
- A resident employee working outside SA for more than 183 full days in any 12 months, including a continuous stretch of more than 60 days, has the first R1.25m of foreign employment income exempt. Income above that is taxed in SA, with foreign tax credits available. — [SARS Foreign Employment Income Exemption](https://www.sars.gov.za/individuals/tax-during-all-life-stages-and-events/foreign-employment-income-exemption/); [SARS FAQ PDF](https://www.sars.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/Ops/Guides/LAPD-IT-G29-PIT-FAQs-Foreign-Employment-Income-Exemption.pdf); [Interpretation Note 16](https://www.sars.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/Legal/Notes/LAPD-IntR-IN-2012-16-Exemption-Foreign-Employment-Income.pdf)
- People who stop being tax resident must tell SARS on the RAV01 form. — [SARS Cease to be resident](https://www.sars.gov.za/individuals/cease-to-be-an-sa-tax-resident-and-reinstatement-of-sa-tax-resident/)
- SA receives offshore account data automatically under the CRS. The Single Discretionary Allowance rose to R2m per calendar year. — [SARS Budget 2026 FAQ](https://www.sars.gov.za/about/sars-tax-and-customs-system/budget/budget-2026-frequently-asked-questions/)

**Deceased estates**
- The SARS Estate Duty page describes the DEC letter, SARS's confirmation to the Master that the deceased's tax obligations are settled. — [SARS Estate Duty](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/estate-duty/)
- The death CGT exclusion is R440,000, and the interest exemption for deceased estates is R23,800. — [SARS Budget 2026 FAQ](https://www.sars.gov.za/about/sars-tax-and-customs-system/budget/budget-2026-frequently-asked-questions/)
- Annual donations tax exemption: R150,000 for individuals (was R100,000). Donations between spouses are exempt. — [SARS Budget 2026 FAQ](https://www.sars.gov.za/about/sars-tax-and-customs-system/budget/budget-2026-frequently-asked-questions/)

### Inferences
- Lesson 1 (brackets) and lesson 4 (deductions/credits) need their figures refreshed to 2026/27. Budget 2026 was the first inflation adjustment since 2023/24.
- Several gap topics can be merged for a short course. A "Saving and investing tax" lesson can cover interest exemption, TFSA and CGT annual exclusion. A "Side income" lesson can extend lesson 5 to cover gig work, crypto and turnover tax. A "When life changes" lesson can cover working abroad and deceased estates.
- The two-pot topic is high-value. People withdrew expecting a fixed deduction and then received auto-assessment bills, which is exactly the lesson-worthy surprise.
- VAT is mostly a business topic. For individuals, one paragraph is enough: 15% is built into prices, and registration is required above R2.3m.

### Gaps
- Exact dates for Filing Season 2026 and auto-assessment came only from the FAQ's two-pot answer (around 21 July 2026 issue date, 20 October 2026 payment date). The Annual Notice itself was not fetched.
- The resident dividends withholding rate (20%) was not confirmed on a page fetched this session. Only the non-resident rate was stated.
- Some fuel levy figures on the SARS FAQ page are garbled ("Petrol 10/litre"). This is not relevant to lessons, but it shows the page has errors, so cross-check key numbers against the [Budget Tax Guide 2026 PDF](https://www.sars.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/Docs/Budget/Budget2026/Budget-tax-guide-2026-web-version.pdf).
- No SARS page on deceased-estate income tax steps (the ITR12 up to date of death, then the IT-AE) was fetched. Treat this as unverified.

## (b1) UK tax course: prioritised lesson list

### Takeaway
Recommended order: (1) Personal allowance and how bands work; (2) Your payslip: PAYE, tax codes and National Insurance; (3) Student loan repayments; (4) Side income: the £1,000 trading and property allowances, and when you must register for Self Assessment; (5) Self Assessment and Making Tax Digital (mandatory from 6 Apr 2026 above £50k); (6) ISAs and tax-free savings; (7) HMRC scams. Thresholds are frozen at £12,570 until 2031, which is why "fiscal drag" is worth teaching explicitly.

### Cited Findings
- The 2026/27 personal allowance is £12,570. Basic rate is 20% to £50,270, higher rate 40% to £125,140, and additional rate 45% above that. The allowance falls by £1 for every £2 over £100,000, reaching zero at £125,140. — [gov.uk Income Tax rates](https://www.gov.uk/income-tax-rates)
- The personal allowance has been fixed since 2021/22 and is due to stay at £12,570 until 2030/31. All rates and thresholds are unchanged from 2025/26. Marriage allowance transfer is £1,260. — [Commons Library CBP-10618](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10618/)
- The first £1,000 of self-employment income (trading allowance) and the first £1,000 of property income are tax-free. — [gov.uk Income Tax rates](https://www.gov.uk/income-tax-rates)
- Employee National Insurance (category A) for 2026/27 is 0% from £129 to £242/week, 8% from £242.01 to £967, and 2% above £967. Employer NI is 15% above £96/week. — [gov.uk NI rates](https://www.gov.uk/national-insurance-rates-letters); [Commons Library](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10618/)
- The NI primary threshold is £12,570/yr and the upper earnings limit £50,270. The lower earnings limit is £6,708/yr: earning above it builds State Pension entitlement even though no NI is paid until the primary threshold. — [gov.uk Rates and thresholds for employers 2026–27](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027)
- Student loan thresholds for 2026/27: Plan 1 £26,900; Plan 2 £29,385; Plan 4 (Scotland) £33,795; Plan 5 £25,000. The rate is 9% of earnings above the threshold. Postgraduate loans are 6% above £21,000. — [gov.uk Rates and thresholds 2026–27](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027); [LITRG student loan repayments](https://www.litrg.org.uk/tax-nic/student-finance-and-tax-system/student-loan-repayments)
- Unearned income above £2,000 counts towards student loan repayment if you file a return. This is "all or nothing": once over £2,000, the whole amount counts. — [LITRG](https://www.litrg.org.uk/tax-nic/student-finance-and-tax-system/student-loan-repayments)
- Making Tax Digital for Income Tax applies from 6 April 2026 if gross income from self-employment plus property is over £50,000. The threshold falls to £30,000 (from 2027) and then £20,000 (from 2028). It requires digital records and quarterly updates. — [gov.uk Sign up for MTD](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/sign-up-for-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax); [LITRG](https://www.litrg.org.uk/tax-nic/making-tax-digital-income-tax/when-does-making-tax-digital-start-me); [ICAEW TAXguide 04/25](https://www.icaew.com/technical/tax/tax-faculty/taxguides/2025/taxguide-04-25)
- MTD applies from April 2026 if 2024/25 qualifying income was over £50k, even if no HMRC letter arrived. — [LITRG](https://www.litrg.org.uk/tax-nic/making-tax-digital-income-tax/when-does-making-tax-digital-start-me)
- The ISA allowance is £20,000 for 2026/27. Budget 2025 legislated a cut to £12,000 in the cash ISA limit for under-65s from 6 April 2027 ([gov.uk policy paper](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/reduction-in-the-cash-individual-savings-account-isa-limit/cash-individual-savings-account-isa-limit-reduction)). **Contradicted:** reports say the 28 Oct 2026 Autumn Budget scrapped that cut, so the cash ISA limit stays at £20,000 — [MoneySavingExpert](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/news/2026/10/cash-isa-limit-cut-cancelled/); [Morningstar](https://global.morningstar.com/en-gb/personal-finance/autumn-budget-what-cash-isa-u-turn-means-your-savings); [Reuters (pre-budget FT report)](https://www.reuters.com/world/uk/uks-healey-reverse-cuts-cash-isa-allowances-ft-reports-2026-10-27/). The gov.uk page has not yet been updated to match.

### Inferences
- The UK course's anchor concept is the band diagram plus the tax code (1257L = £12,570 allowance). The most common real-world mistakes are emergency tax codes on a first job and second jobs taking their allowance as "BR".
- Student loans work like a 9% extra marginal "tax", so a Plan 2 graduate's effective marginal rate is 20% + 8% + 9% = 37%. This is worth showing explicitly. (The arithmetic comes from the figures above.)
- MTD matters mainly to landlords and sole traders, but young side-hustlers will cross £20k from 2028. Teach "keep digital records from day one".

### Gaps
- Not fetched: the Self Assessment registration deadline (5 October after the tax year), the filing/payment deadline of 31 January and payments on account. Verify these at gov.uk/self-assessment-tax-returns before writing.
- HMRC scam guidance was not fetched. gov.uk has a "Report suspicious HMRC emails, text messages and phone calls" page (the known channels are phishing@hmrc.gov.uk and texts to 60599), but this was not verified this session.
- Other Autumn Budget 2026 (28 Oct 2026) measures, such as threshold changes for 2027/28, were not reviewed.
- Scottish income tax bands (six bands) were not captured. A UK course needs a Scotland sidebar.
- No UK-specific tax literacy survey was found.

## (b2) US tax course: prioritised lesson list

### Takeaway
Recommended order: (1) First job paperwork: W-4, W-2 vs 1099 and withholding; (2) How brackets work: marginal vs average rate, filing status and standard deduction; (3) Refunds aren't free money: credits (EITC, CTC) vs deductions; (4) Filing for free: Free File, VITA and Fillable Forms (Direct File is gone); (5) Federal vs state tax; (6) Gig work: self-employment tax and quarterly estimated taxes; (7) New 2025–2028 deductions (tips, overtime, seniors, car loan interest); (8) IRS scams and ghost preparers. Tax Foundation polling shows bracket mechanics and deductions-vs-credits are the biggest knowledge gaps, especially among low earners.

### Cited Findings
- Tax year 2026 standard deduction: $16,100 single or married filing separately, $32,200 married filing jointly, $24,150 head of household. — [IRS Working Families Tax Cuts](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/working-families-tax-cuts-individuals-and-workers)
- 2026 brackets for single filers: 10% up to $12,400; 12% above $12,400; 22% above $50,400; 24% above $105,700; 32% above $201,775; 35% above $256,225; 37% above $640,600. Married filing jointly thresholds are double up to the 35% band. — [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/working-families-tax-cuts-individuals-and-workers)
- New deductions for 2025–2028: qualified tips up to $25,000; the overtime premium (the "half" in time-and-a-half) up to $12,500 ($25,000 joint); seniors 65+ an extra $6,000; car loan interest up to $10,000 on new US-assembled vehicles. All phase out at higher incomes and all require an SSN. — [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/working-families-tax-cuts-individuals-and-workers)
- The Child Tax Credit is up to $2,200 per qualifying child, of which up to $1,700 is refundable depending on income. — [IRS Child Tax Credit](https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/individuals/child-tax-credit); [IRS family benefits](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/tax-benefits-for-parents-and-families)
- To get the refundable additional CTC you need at least $2,500 of earned income. — [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/the-child-tax-credit-benefits-eligible-parents)
- Maximum EITC for 2026 is $8,231 (3+ children). The other amounts are in Rev. Proc. 2025-32. — [IRS IR-2025-103](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-releases-tax-inflation-adjustments-for-tax-year-2026-including-amendments-from-the-one-big-beautiful-bill)
- The child and dependent care credit rate rises to 50% from tax year 2026. — [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/individuals/child-and-dependent-care-credit-information)
- IRS Direct File was not available in the 2026 filing season. The IRS told states it "will not be available", and the former Commissioner called it "gone". — [Federal News Network](https://federalnewsnetwork.com/it-modernization/2025/11/irs-direct-file-will-not-be-available-in-2026-agency-tells-states/); [NATP](https://www.natptax.com/news-insights/blog/direct-file-is-gone-what-s-next-for-free-filing-and-tax-pros/)
- Free options in 2026 were: IRS Free File (AGI $89,000 or less, 8 partners); Free File Fillable Forms (any income); MilTax; and VITA/TCE volunteer prep. Refunds typically arrive within 21 days with e-file plus direct deposit. — [IRS Tax Tip 2026-08](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/2026-tax-filing-season-opens-with-several-free-filing-options-available)
- The 2026 Dirty Dozen scams list includes IRS impersonation by text, email and QR code; AI voice-clone robocalls; viral "tax hacks"; the fake "self-employment tax credit"; ghost preparers who won't sign or give a PTIN; and fabricated withholding. The IRS usually makes first contact by mail. Report phishing to phishing@irs.gov and other fraud at IRS.gov/SubmitATip. The IP PIN locks your account against fraudulent returns. — [IRS IR-2026-30](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/dirty-dozen-tax-scams-for-2026-irs-reminds-taxpayers-to-watch-out-for-dangerous-threats); [Key Private Bank summary](https://www.key.com/content/dam/kco/documents/wealth_management/dirty-dozen-tax-scams.pdf)
- Non-itemizers can deduct charitable gifts from 2026 (up to $1,000 single / $2,000 joint). — [Ameriprise](https://www.ameriprise.com/financial-goals-priorities/taxes/one-big-beautiful-bill-tax-cuts) (secondary source)
- The 2026 IRA contribution limit is $7,500 ($8,600 at age 50+). — [TurboTax](https://turbotax.intuit.com/tax-tips/tax-deductions-and-credits/tax-deductions-2020-what-will-sunset-or-change/L7gdLfrub) (secondary source)

### Inferences
- "Will a raise push me into a higher bracket and lose me money?" is the classic misconception and should be lesson 2's hook (see the survey section).
- The free-filing lesson must be dated each year. Direct File is gone, so Free File's $89k AGI ceiling and VITA are the main zero-cost routes.
- Because the tips and overtime deductions expire after 2028, lessons should be labelled "2025–2028 only".

### Gaps
- Self-employment tax (15.3% on 92.35% of net earnings, $400 filing threshold), quarterly estimated tax dates (15 Apr, 15 Jun, 15 Sep, 15 Jan) and the 1099-K threshold (back to $20,000 / 200 transactions under OBBBA, per the [TurboTax video transcript](https://turbotax.intuit.com/tax-tips/videos/video-big-beautiful-bill-2026-tax-law-changes-that-affect-your-wallet---presented-by-thestreet-turbotax/c5x1eJBpR)) were not confirmed on irs.gov this session. Use IRS Topic 554, Form 1040-ES and the IRS 1099-K page.
- W-4 and W-2 mechanics were not fetched. Use irs.gov/w4 and the Tax Withholding Estimator, which the IRS says reflects the OBBB changes ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/working-families-tax-cuts-individuals-and-workers)).
- No state revenue department pages were researched. Nine states have no wage income tax, but this was not verified this session.
- EITC amounts for 0, 1 and 2 children in 2026 were not extracted.

## (c) What surveys say people wish they knew about tax

### Takeaway
Direct "what do you wish you'd learned about tax" survey data is thin. The best evidence is (1) strong demand for school personal finance, (2) Tax Foundation polling showing that bracket mechanics and deductions-vs-credits are poorly understood, especially by low earners and those with less education, and (3) low general financial literacy. No SA or UK tax-literacy survey was found.

### Cited Findings
- Tax Foundation / Public Policy Polling (Dec 2024, final sample 2,064). The share answering correctly on how brackets work was about 65% for under-45s, 46% for over-65s, 66% for those earning $100–150k and only 28% for those under $25k. On deductions vs credits, it was 47% for postgraduates vs 24% for high-school-only respondents. Only 22% knew the top 1%'s share of the tax burden. — [Tax Foundation](https://taxfoundation.org/research/all/federal/us-tax-literacy-poll-knowledge-perceptions/)
- NEFE poll (March 2025, 800 US adults): 82% wish they had been required to take a personal finance class, 83% want it required for graduation, and 61% say their school didn't offer one. — [NEFE](https://www.nefe.org/news/2025/04/poll-majority-of-us-adults-want-financial-education-in-high-schools.aspx)
- Only 29% of US adults answered all three basic financial literacy questions correctly. — [Stanford IFDM](https://ifdm.stanford.edu/data-financial-literacy)
- A TurboTax survey found 2 in 5 Americans knew little about the 2025 tax bill. — [TurboTax video transcript](https://turbotax.intuit.com/tax-tips/videos/video-big-beautiful-bill-2026-tax-law-changes-that-affect-your-wallet---presented-by-thestreet-turbotax/c5x1eJBpR) (vendor claim, methodology not given)

### Inferences
- Lesson priorities supported by the evidence are marginal-rate mechanics and deductions vs credits. Both are biggest for low earners, who are the course's likely audience.

### Gaps
- No SA (e.g. FSCA or SARS) or UK (e.g. MaPS or LITRG) tax-literacy survey was located in the search budget.

## (d) Licence and reuse terms of authoritative sources

### Takeaway
IRS content is US federal government work and is generally public domain. gov.uk/HMRC content is under the Open Government Licence v3.0, which allows reuse with attribution. SARS reuse terms were not verified: paraphrase and link, don't copy.

### Cited Findings
- 17 U.S.C. § 105 places works prepared by US Government employees in the public domain. — [askGPO](https://ask.gpo.gov/s/article/What-are-the-copyright-and-use-policies-of-govinfo-content); [ARL issue brief](https://www.arl.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/copyright-status-of-government-works.pdf)
- Third-party sites attribute UK public sector information as "licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0". — [EU Access2Markets copyright notice](https://trade.ec.europa.eu/access-to-markets/en/services-copyright)

### Inferences
- IRS text and figures can be adapted freely. Avoid IRS logos and anything implying endorsement, and note that some IRS pages embed third-party material.
- For gov.uk use the attribution line: "Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0". OGL excludes logos and crests.
- LITRG, ICAEW, Tax Foundation, NEFE, MoneySavingExpert and the banks are copyrighted. Cite and link them, don't copy.
