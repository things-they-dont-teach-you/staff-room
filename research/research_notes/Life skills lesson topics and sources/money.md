# Money (personal finance, not income tax): lesson topics and sources — SA first, then UK, US

Researched 2026-11-04. Snippet-level checks via web search (about 15 searches); few pages were fully fetched. Re-check every number against the primary source before publishing a lesson.

## Prioritised candidate lesson list (synthesis)

Priority is based on (a) gaps shown in survey data (below), (b) harm if the topic goes untaught (debt traps, scams), and (c) whether a primary source exists to cite.

1. **Compound interest, in both directions (saving vs debt)**: the biggest measured knowledge gap (only 42% answer correctly; see Q5).
2. **Budgeting and an emergency fund**: only 43% could cover 3 months of expenses (Q5).
3. **Borrowing costs and legal caps**: SA NCR caps, initiation and service fees, store cards, mashonisas, payday loans.
4. **Credit reports and scores**: free reports in SA, UK and US. Fewer than 5% of South Africans use theirs.
5. **Scams and Ponzi schemes**: check the regulator's register (FSCA/FCA) first. 15% of adults have been scammed.
6. **Getting out of debt**: SA debt review (NCA s86) and where to get free help in the UK and US.
7. **Retirement savings**: SA two-pot, RAs, TFSAs; UK ISAs/pensions; US 401(k)/IRA.
8. **Inflation and index funds/unit trusts**: risk vs reward and fees.
9. **Consumer rights and contracts**: SA CPA (cooling-off, 6-month defective-goods return), NCC, ombuds.
10. **Payslips and UIF**: what is deducted and what UIF pays out.
11. **Renting and leases**: SA Rental Housing Act deposit rules.
12. **Insurance basics and buying a car**: no primary sources gathered yet (see Gaps).

## Q1: Budgeting, emergency funds, bank fees, compound interest, credit scores and reports

### Takeaway
Free credit reports are a legal right in all three countries: once a year per bureau in SA under the NCA, and weekly in the US through AnnualCreditReport.com. Almost nobody in SA uses this right. Compound interest is the concept adults most often get wrong.

### Cited Findings
- SA: you are entitled to a free copy of your credit report from each registered credit bureau once a year. — [Credit Bureau Association](https://www.cba.co.za/bureaus/); [NCR brochure](https://ncr.org.za/documents/pages/Brochures/NCR_%20english%20.pdf)
- SA: "Fewer than 5% of South African consumers make use of the legislation which entitles them to obtain their credit report free of charge from every credit bureau every year." — [TransUnion SA](https://www.transunion.co.za/assistance/free-credit-report)
- SA: the NCA gives the right to dispute factually incorrect information on a bureau report and have it corrected. — [Experian SA](https://www.experian.co.za/consumer/my-free-credit-check-and-your-free-credit-report)
- US: everyone is entitled by law to one free report every 12 months from each of Equifax, Experian and TransUnion. The bureaus have **permanently** extended free **weekly** reports at AnnualCreditReport.com (made permanent in Oct 2023). — [FTC consumer alert](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2023/10/you-now-have-permanent-access-free-weekly-credit-reports); [FTC: Free Credit Reports](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/free-credit-reports)
- US: the official route is AnnualCreditReport.com or 1-877-322-8228. — [CFPB](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/how-do-i-get-a-free-copy-of-my-credit-reports-en-5/); [AnnualCreditReport.com](https://www.annualcreditreport.com/index.action)
- Credit reports can also reveal identity theft. — [FTC consumer alert](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2023/10/you-now-have-permanent-access-free-weekly-credit-reports)

### Inferences
- A lesson activity of "request your free report today" works in all three countries. In SA it addresses a right that 95%+ of people don't use.
- Teach US learners to use AnnualCreditReport.com only. Lookalike "free report" sites are a known trap (the FTC and CFPB point only to the official site).

### Gaps
- UK statutory credit report (free from Experian/Equifax/TransUnion under UK GDPR subject-access and the Consumer Credit Act) was **not verified** this session. Check ICO/FCA pages.
- SA bank fees: no authoritative comparison found (searched indirectly). The Banking Association SA consumer pages are a possible source.
- No authoritative source collected for an emergency-fund rule of thumb. OECD data (Q5) shows the gap but gives no prescription.

## Q2: Debt — credit cards, store cards, personal loans, mashonisas, rate caps, debt review, payday loans

### Takeaway
SA caps interest by formula linked to the repo rate, fixed at the date the credit is granted. Unsecured credit is capped at repo + 21%, credit and store cards at repo + 14%, and short-term loans at 5%/month on the first loan. Initiation fees and a R60/month service fee are also capped. Over-indebted consumers can apply for debt review under NCA s86.

### Cited Findings
- NCR Table A maximum rates (RR = SARB repo rate): mortgages RR+12%/yr; credit facilities (credit and store cards) RR+14%/yr; unsecured credit RR+21%/yr; developmental credit RR+27%/yr; short-term credit 5%/month on the first loan and 3%/month on subsequent loans in a calendar year; other credit agreements RR+17%/yr; incidental credit 2%/month. — [Regulations: Review of limitations of fees and interest rates (GG 39379, gov.za)](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201511/39379gon1080.pdf); summary with 2026 review date at [MJK Inc (law firm, secondary)](https://mjkinc.co.za/finance-credit-law/interest-and-the-in-duplum-rule)
- The cap is fixed at the repo rate in force when the credit is granted. — [Banking Association SA](https://banking.org.za/consumer-information/consumer-information-legislation/national-credit-act/); [MJK Inc](https://mjkinc.co.za/finance-credit-law/interest-and-the-in-duplum-rule)
- Maximum initiation fee for unsecured, short-term and credit-facility agreements: R165 plus 10% of the amount above R1,000, capped at R1,050. Mortgages: R1,100 plus 10% of the amount above R1,000, capped at R5,250. Maximum monthly service fee: R60 (excluding VAT). — [GG 39379](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201511/39379gon1080.pdf)
- Prescribed (mora) rate of interest: 10.25% p.a. from 1 March 2026 (repo + 3.5%). By implication the repo rate was 6.75% then. — [GenN 3887, GG 54520](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202604/54520gen3887.pdf) (via MJK Inc)
- Debt review: an over-indebted consumer may apply voluntarily to a debt counsellor under NCA s86. If the consumer is found not over-indebted, the counsellor must reject the application (s86(7)(a)). The NCR publishes guidelines for withdrawing from debt review. — [SciELO SA (PER journal)](https://scielo.org.za/scielo.php?script=sci_arttext&pid=S2225-71602013000400009); [NCR withdrawal guidelines 2021 (mirror)](https://legalrights.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/National-Credit-Regulators-Withdrawal-from-Debt-Review-Guidelines-2021.pdf)
- The registration threshold for credit providers is now R0, so every lender must register with the NCR. — [MJK Inc (secondary)](https://mjkinc.co.za/finance-credit-law/interest-and-the-in-duplum-rule)

### Inferences
- Worked example for a lesson, at an assumed repo rate of 6.75%: unsecured loan cap ≈ 27.75%/yr and store card cap ≈ 20.75%/yr. The short-term cap of 5%/month on the first loan is about 60%/yr simple interest before fees. Recompute this with the live repo rate when publishing.
- Mashonisas: because the registration threshold is nil, an unregistered lender is operating illegally, so "ask for their NCR registration number" is a teachable check. Confirm wording against NCR guidance.

### Gaps
- No NCR primary page found that names mashonisas or loan sharks, or explains how to verify a registration. Check ncr.org.za's registrant search.
- Current SARB repo rate (Nov 2026) not retrieved directly.
- UK payday loan cap (FCA high-cost short-term credit price cap) and US state payday rules: not searched. FCA and CFPB pages are needed.
- Credit card minimum-payment trap: no primary source gathered (CFPB has material on this).

## Q3: Saving and investing — retirement, TFSAs/ISAs/401(k)/IRA, inflation, index funds, scams

### Takeaway
SA's two-pot system (from 1 Sept 2024) allows one taxed withdrawal per tax year from the savings pot. SA TFSA annual limit rose to R46,000 from 1 March 2026 (lifetime R500,000, 40% penalty on excess). The UK cash ISA cut to £12,000 was **cancelled** in the 28 Oct 2026 Budget, so £20,000 remains. US 2026 limits are $24,500 for a 401(k) and $7,500 for an IRA.

### Cited Findings
- SA two-pot: from 1 Sept 2024, 10% of the vested pot, capped at R30,000, was moved into the savings pot as seed capital. One-third of contributions from 1 Sept 2024 go to the savings pot and the savings pot can be accessed once a year. — [FSCA two-pot explainer (PDF)](https://www.fsca.co.za/_api/cr3ad_shareddocumentses(d36a1ed9-0c6b-f111-a826-000d3a64f818)/cr3ad_document/$value); [FSCA two-pot FAQ (PDF)](https://www.fsca.co.za/_api/cr3ad_shareddocumentses(23b8ded9-0c6b-f111-a826-7c1e5277e2e0)/cr3ad_document/$value)
- Withdrawal needs a minimum savings-pot balance of R2,000, is limited to once per tax year, and is taxed at your marginal rate. — [Nedbank FAQ](https://personal.nedbank.co.za/learn/blog/2-pot-savings-withdrawals-faqs.html); [Momentum (PDF)](https://sls-fresco.momentum.co.za/files/documents/corporate/two-pot/english/two-pot-withdrawal-fees-and-tax-implications.pdf); official estimator: [SARS savings pot calculator](https://tools.sars.gov.za/sarsonlinequery/savings-pot-calculator)
- SA TFSA: annual limit increased from R36,000 to R46,000 from 1 March 2026 (Budget 25 Feb 2026). The lifetime limit stays at R500,000. There is a 40% "penalty" (as normal tax) on any excess, e.g. investing R50,000 gives R4,000 × 40% = R1,600. — [SARS Tax Free Investments](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/personal-income-tax/tax-free-investments/)
- UK: the 2025 Budget planned to cut the cash ISA limit to £12,000 for under-65s from 6 Apr 2027, inside a £20,000 overall limit. — [GOV.UK policy paper](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/reduction-in-the-cash-individual-savings-account-isa-limit/cash-individual-savings-account-isa-limit-reduction). **Superseded:** the Autumn Budget on 28 Oct 2026 scrapped the cut, so the cash ISA limit stays at £20,000 for all ages. A "starter investment ISA" was announced. — [MoneySavingExpert](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/news/2026/10/cash-isa-limit-cut-cancelled/); [Morningstar UK](https://global.morningstar.com/en-gb/personal-finance/autumn-budget-what-cash-isa-u-turn-means-your-savings); [NatWest update 28 Oct 2026](https://www.natwest.com/savings/isa-overview/autumn-budget.html). Note: some pages (Starling, MoneySupermarket) still show the £12k cut. The GOV.UK page above had not been checked for a withdrawal notice.
- US 2026: employee 401(k)/403(b)/457/TSP limit $24,500; catch-up for ages 50+ $8,000 (total $32,500); ages 60–63 catch-up $11,250; IRA limit $7,500; IRA catch-up for 50+ $1,100. — [IRS IR-2025-111](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/401k-limit-increases-to-24500-for-2026-ira-limit-increases-to-7500); [IRS COLA table](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/cola-increases-for-dollar-limitations-on-benefits-and-contributions)
- SA scams: the FSCA publishes ongoing warnings about entities "unlawfully conducting unauthorised financial services business" and about people impersonating the FSCA or licensed FSPs (e.g. clones of real FSP numbers). — [FSCA home, Warnings and Alerts](https://www.fsca.co.za/); [FSCA Facebook #WarningWednesdays](https://www.facebook.com/fscasouthafrica/posts/warningwednesdaysthe-fsca-warns-the-public-against-the-following-individuals-or-/853376413492125/)
- UK equivalent: the FCA Warning List of unauthorised firms says if you deal with one "it's unlikely you'd get your money back". — [FCA Warning List](https://www.fca.org.uk/consumers/warning-list-unauthorised-firms)

### Inferences
- Lesson framing for two-pot: early withdrawal costs you twice, once in tax at your marginal rate and once in lost compounding. This links to Lesson 1. Tax mechanics stay brief because a separate course covers tax.
- Scam lesson: "check the register, and check the contact details match the register" (clone firms reuse real FSP numbers).

### Gaps
- SA retirement annuity (RA) rules, unit trust/index fund basics and fee impact: no primary source gathered (FSCA/ASISA material is likely).
- UK pension auto-enrolment minimums and the current overall ISA rules page on GOV.UK: not fetched.
- Investor.gov (SEC) compound interest calculator and index fund pages: not fetched, though they are a known public-domain US resource.
- No SA inflation (CPI) source retrieved (Stats SA/SARB).

## Q4: Insurance, contracts and consumer rights, renting, buying a car, payslips, UIF

### Takeaway
The SA CPA gives a 5-business-day cooling-off right for direct marketing and lets consumers return unsafe or defective goods within 6 months. UIF pays a sliding scale of 38–60% of earnings. Each 4 days worked earns 1 credit day, up to 365.

### Cited Findings
- SA CPA (Act 68 of 2008): direct-marketing transactions carry a 5-business-day cooling-off period. Consumers may return goods of inferior quality, unsafe or defective, within 6 months of delivery, at the supplier's risk and expense. Rights listed include equality, privacy, choice, disclosure, fair and responsible marketing, and fair value, good quality and safety. — [dtic CPA booklet (PDF)](https://www.thedtic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/Consumer_Protection_Act.pdf)
- SA UIF: you earn 1 credit day for every 4 days of contributions, up to 365. For credit days 0–238 the benefit follows a sliding scale of 38%–60% of earnings; days 239–365 are paid at a flat 20%. Worked example: daily benefit = daily remuneration × 38%. — [Dept of Employment and Labour, UIF Easy Aid guide (PDF)](https://www.labour.gov.za/DocumentCenter/Publications/Unemployment%20Insurance%20Fund/EASY%20AID%20Guide%20Spreadsheet%20Application%202.pdf); [DEL fact sheet on calculating UIF benefit (PDF)](https://www.labour.gov.za/DocumentCenter/Publications/Unemployment%20Insurance%20Fund/Fact%20Sheet%20on%20the%20Calculation%20of%20normal%20UIF%20benefit.pdf)
- SA Rental Housing Act 50 of 1999: the deposit must be returned with interest after a proper inspection. — [Ikhaya News (secondary, weak)](https://www.facebook.com/IkhayaNewsChannel/videos/tenant-tipyour-landlord-cannot-just-keep-your-deposit-the-rental-housing-act-50-/2323373321494732/). One forum post claims a refund within 7 days if there are no deductions, but this is **unverified**. — [Facebook group (weak)](https://www.facebook.com/groups/207494545268650/posts/931049529579811/)

### Inferences
- The payslip lesson can pair the UIF deduction with what it buys (the benefit scale) to make the deduction meaningful.

### Gaps
- Rental Housing Act s5 deposit rules (interest-bearing account; 7/14/21-day refund timelines; joint inspection) were **not verified from the Act text**. Fetch the Act from gov.za before teaching the timelines.
- UIF contribution rate (1% employee + 1% employer, with a ceiling) was not confirmed in a primary source this session.
- National Consumer Commission complaint process, ombud offices (Ombudsman for Banking Services, Credit Ombud, Motor Industry Ombud, FAIS Ombud) and their contact routes were not researched.
- Insurance basics and buying a car (e.g. balloon payments, NCA disclosure on vehicle finance) had no sources gathered.
- UK/US equivalents (Consumer Rights Act 2015, FTC Cooling-Off Rule of 3 days for sales of $25+) were not verified, except one municipal page citing the FTC rule. — [Wheeling IL](https://www.wheelingil.gov/209/The-Cooling-Off-Rule) (secondary; cite FTC directly instead).

## Q5: Financial literacy survey data showing gaps

### Takeaway
SA's FSCA 2020 baseline put average financial literacy at 52/100, down across all domains. Across 39 countries, OECD/INFE 2023 found only 42% understand compound interest and only 43% could survive 3 months without income.

### Cited Findings
- FSCA 2020 Financial Literacy Baseline Survey (launched 28 July 2022; n=2,693 adults aged 16+, nationally representative): average score 52/100. All domains (Financial Control, Financial Planning, Product Choice, Financial Knowledge) declined, attributed to unemployment, lower GDP per capita and Covid-19. — [FSCA press release](https://www.fsca.co.za/_api/cr3ad_newses(418d9578-f85f-f111-a826-000d3a64fd63)/cr3ad_document/$value)
- In the same survey, about 36% managed household finances themselves and about a third made decisions jointly. — [HSRC output](https://hsrc.ac.za/outputs/13691/)
- The FSCA's media release said SA "ranked last out of 19 countries" in a recent poll of global adult financial literacy. That poll is not named in the snippet. — [FSCA Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/fscasouthafrica/posts/media-releasethe-fsca-launches-baseline-survey-results-on-financial-literacy-in-/414533117376459/)
- OECD/INFE 2023 (39 countries; data 2022/23): average financial literacy 60/100 (OECD 63); only 34% reach the 70-point target. 84% understand inflation but only 63% can apply the time value of money. 77% understand risk vs reward but only 42% answer compound interest correctly, and only 46% of savings-product holders do. Only 26% compare products across providers and 24% seek independent advice. 15% have been a victim of a scam. Only 54% could pay a month's-income expense without borrowing, 43% could cover 3 months' living costs, and 38% have money left at month end. Only 37% check whether a provider is regulated. 41% know crypto is not legal tender. — [OECD/INFE 2023 report (PDF)](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2023/12/oecd-infe-2023-international-survey-of-adult-financial-literacy_8ce94e2c/56003a32-en.pdf); [landing page](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-infe-2023-international-survey-of-adult-financial-literacy_56003a32-en.html)
- About two out of three scam victims don't reach the minimum literacy target. — [OECD/INFE 2023](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2023/12/oecd-infe-2023-international-survey-of-adult-financial-literacy_8ce94e2c/56003a32-en.pdf)

### Inferences
- The data directly supports ordering the lessons: compound interest, then emergency fund, then comparing products and checking the regulator, then scams.
- SA was not in the OECD 2023 country list snippet, so SA comparisons should use the FSCA baseline.

### Gaps
- S&P Global FinLit Survey (2014): not retrieved this session. Don't cite SA's figure from memory.
- No FSCA survey newer than 2020 was found. A later wave may exist.
- UK (MaPS) and US (FINRA NFCS) national surveys were not retrieved.
