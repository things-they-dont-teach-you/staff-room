# Research

Background research behind the course plans. Each report in `reports/` is built from the notes
in the matching `research_notes/` folder.

- [Life skills lesson topics and sources](reports/Life%20skills%20lesson%20topics%20and%20sources.md):
  which lessons each subject needs, and free sources to cite.
- [Retirement and investing lessons](reports/Retirement%20and%20investing%20lessons.md):
  the investing courses, per country and global.

Notes were gathered in November 2026 and are not checked facts. Before a figure goes into a
course, confirm it on the official source and record it in that course's `facts.md`. Mind the
licence notes: some sources may be linked but not copied into tutor material.
