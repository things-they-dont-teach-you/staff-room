# UK retirement and investing basics (2026/27, as of 5 Nov 2026)

Scope: key facts, current figures and free authoritative sources, with reuse terms, for a prioritised UK beginner lesson list. Tax year 2026/27 = 6 Apr 2026 to 5 Apr 2027. The 28 Oct 2026 Autumn Budget (Chancellor John Healey, PM Andy Burnham) changed several items below. Most Budget measures still need legislation and may change.

Source note: items marked "(not fetched)" are stable gov.uk/regulator pages cited from prior knowledge and not re-read this session. Verify the figure before publishing.

## 1. State Pension

### Takeaway
Full new State Pension is £241.30 a week (£12,547.60 a year) in 2026/27 and rises to £250.70 a week (£13,036.40) in April 2027. The triple lock stays until April 2030, then becomes an "adjusted" triple lock. State Pension age is rising from 66 to 67 between April 2026 and April 2028.

### Cited Findings
- Full new State Pension 2026/27: £241.30 a week. — [GOV.UK: What you'll get](https://www.gov.uk/new-state-pension/what-youll-get); [Commons Library CBP-10403](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10403/)
- Up from £230.25 in 2025/26 (a 4.8% rise). — [People's Pension](https://www.peoplespension.co.uk/guides-and-insights/latest-updates/guides/all/understanding-the-state-pension/)
- £12,547.60 a year, £22.40 under the £12,570 personal allowance. — [Sky News](https://news.sky.com/story/is-major-pension-change-coming-and-will-burnham-listen-to-own-advisers-who-hate-triple-lock-13559422)
- From April 2027: new State Pension £250.70 a week (£13,036.40 a year); basic State Pension £192.10 a week (£9,989.20). — [MSE, 28 Oct 2026](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/news/2026/10/budget-state-pension-tax-free-allowance-rise/). Rathbones gives the rise as 3.9%, to £13,036. — [Rathbones](https://www.rathbones.com/en-gb/wealth-management/knowledge-and-insight/autumn-budget-2026-what-it-means-for-you)
- New **Pension Tax Allowance** from April 2027: State Pensioners with total income of £13,037 a year or less pay no income tax. It is worth up to £94 a year. Income source does not matter, and you don't need the full new State Pension. — [MSE](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/news/2026/10/budget-state-pension-tax-free-allowance-rise/)
- **Current triple lock:** the pension rises each year by the highest of earnings growth, CPI inflation or 2.5%. — [IFS](https://ifs.org.uk/articles/how-will-new-triple-lock-work-and-what-effects-will-it-have)
- **Adjusted triple lock from April 2030:** the basic and new State Pension rise by the highest of CPI, 2.5%, or "the amount required to return or maintain the value of the State Pension relative to earnings". That means it tracks earnings over time, not every year. — [GOV.UK Triple Lock reform factsheet](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/state-pension-uprating-analysis-2026/triple-lock-reform-factsheet); [IFS](https://ifs.org.uk/articles/how-will-new-triple-lock-work-and-what-effects-will-it-have)
- The government says the change saves £15bn a year by the end of the 2030s. — [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c63reg9dnex9o); [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/uk/why-is-burnham-unwinding-uks-triple-lock-state-pension-pledge-2026-09-29/)
- Sources describe the reform differently. Rathbones calls it a "double lock" and says "the link to average earnings would end". GOV.UK and the IFS say an earnings catch-up element remains. **Use the GOV.UK factsheet wording.** — [Rathbones](https://www.rathbones.com/en-gb/wealth-management/knowledge-and-insight/triple-lock-could-change-plan-for-retirement) vs [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/state-pension-uprating-analysis-2026/triple-lock-reform-factsheet)
- **State Pension age** started rising from 66 in April 2026 and reaches 67 in April 2028. It is legislated to reach 68 between 2044 and 2046. — [Commons WPC report](https://publications.parliament.uk/pa/cm5902/cmselect/cmworpen/76/report.html); [GOV.UK GAD](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/gad-and-the-state-pension-age-review)
- Qualifying years: you need at least 10 years on your National Insurance record to get any new State Pension, and 35 for the full amount. — [GOV.UK eligibility](https://www.gov.uk/new-state-pension/eligibility) (not fetched)
- Check your forecast and NI record free: [GOV.UK Check your State Pension forecast](https://www.gov.uk/check-state-pension) (not fetched). You can fill gaps with voluntary NI contributions: [GOV.UK voluntary NI](https://www.gov.uk/voluntary-national-insurance-contributions) (not fetched)

### Inferences
- Teach two numbers: the 2026/27 rate (£241.30) and the April 2027 rate (£250.70). Show both with dates, because the lesson will be live across the April switch.
- A young learner's takeaway: State Pension alone is about £12.5k a year, and from 67 or later. So a workplace pension matters.

### Gaps
- Outcome of the third State Pension age review (whether 68 is brought forward) was not found. The Commons committee report refers to a "forthcoming" review.
- The exact uprating formula and any legislation for the adjusted triple lock: the factsheet excerpt only. Read it in full before teaching the details.

## 2. Workplace pensions

### Takeaway
Employers must auto-enrol workers aged 22 to State Pension age earning over £10,000. The legal minimum is 8% of qualifying earnings (£6,240 to £50,270), at least 3% from the employer. From April 2029 only £2,000 a year of salary-sacrificed pension pay will be free of National Insurance. The MoneyHelper Pensions Dashboard isn't open to the public yet (expected 2027/28).

### Cited Findings
- 2026/27 thresholds: earnings trigger £10,000 (£833 a month); qualifying earnings £6,240 to £50,270. All are unchanged from 2025/26. — [TPR earnings thresholds](https://www.thepensionsregulator.gov.uk/en/employers/new-employers/im-an-employer-who-has-to-provide-a-pension/declare-your-compliance/ongoing-duties-for-employers/earnings-thresholds); [GOV.UK 2026/27 review](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/review-of-the-automatic-enrolment-earnings-trigger-and-qualifying-earnings-band-for-202627/review-of-the-automatic-enrolment-earnings-trigger-and-qualifying-earnings-band-for-202627); [Written statement HCWS1206, 18 Dec 2025](https://questions-statements.parliament.uk/written-statements/detail/2025-12-18/hcws1206)
- Auto-enrolment applies to workers aged 22 to State Pension age. The minimum total is 8% of qualifying earnings, at least 3% from the employer. — [MoneyHelper auto-enrolment](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/pensions-and-retirement/pensions-basics/automatic-enrolment-an-introduction) (age/8% part not fetched); [GOV.UK Workplace pensions](https://www.gov.uk/workplace-pensions) (not fetched)
- Opting out: you can opt out after you're enrolled. You get your money back if you opt out within one month. Employers re-enrol you about every 3 years. — [GOV.UK Workplace pensions: stopping](https://www.gov.uk/workplace-pensions/if-you-want-to-leave-your-workplace-pension-scheme) (not fetched)
- **Salary sacrifice (2025 Budget):** from April 2029 only the first £2,000 a year of employee pension contributions made by salary sacrifice is exempt from NICs. Sacrifice above that is still allowed. — [GOV.UK policy paper](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/changes-to-salary-sacrifice-for-pensions-from-april-2029/changes-to-salary-sacrifice-for-pensions-from-april-2029)
- About 15% of employees sacrifice more than £2,000 a year (18% in the private sector, 7% in the public sector). — [IFS](https://ifs.org.uk/publications/assessing-governments-reform-national-insurance-treatment-salary-sacrifice-pension)
- 28 Oct 2026 Budget: I found no reversal of the £2,000 cap. Rathbones' post-Budget note still mentions "a clampdown on salary sacrifice". — [Rathbones](https://www.rathbones.com/en-gb/wealth-management/knowledge-and-insight/autumn-budget-2026-what-it-means-for-you)
- **Pensions dashboards:** schemes must connect by 31 October 2026. The MoneyHelper Pensions Dashboard is expected to open to the public in financial year 2027/28. — [Pensions Dashboards Programme](https://www.pensionsdashboardsprogramme.org.uk/dashboards); [LV=](https://www.lv.com/pension-dashboards); [Burges Salmon, May 2026](https://www.burges-salmon.com/articles/102mx41/pensions-dashboards-update-may-2026/)
- **Finding lost pensions today:** use the free Pension Tracing Service. — [GOV.UK Find pension contact details](https://www.gov.uk/find-pension-contact-details) (not fetched)

### Inferences
- Lesson message: don't opt out. It throws away the employer's 3%, the "free money".
- Salary sacrifice is a footnote for beginners. Mention it as "check whether your employer offers it; NI savings are capped from 2029".

### Gaps
- Whether the 28 Oct 2026 Budget changed salary sacrifice further is not confirmed. I found no primary Budget document on it.
- The Pensions Bill auto-enrolment changes (lowering the age to 18, removing the lower limit) are not checked.

## 3. Personal pensions, SIPPs, tax relief and taking your pension

### Takeaway
Pension contributions get tax relief at your marginal rate. The annual allowance is £60,000 (or 100% of earnings). 25% tax-free cash is unchanged, capped by the £268,275 Lump Sum Allowance. The minimum pension age rises from 55 to 57 on 6 April 2028. Pension Wise gives free guidance from age 50.

### Cited Findings
- The 28 Oct 2026 Budget left pension tax-free cash unchanged: 25% up to the £268,275 Lump Sum Allowance. — [Rathbones](https://www.rathbones.com/en-gb/wealth-management/knowledge-and-insight/autumn-budget-2026-what-it-means-for-you); LSA figure also from [J.P. Morgan Personal Investing](https://www.personalinvesting.jpmorgan.com/guides/pensions-and-retirement/understanding-pension-allowances-and-protections)
- Normal minimum pension age rises to 57 from 6 April 2028 (unless you have a protected pension age). — [GOV.UK Increasing NMPA](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/increasing-normal-minimum-pension-age/increasing-normal-minimum-pension-age); [Fidelity](https://retirement.fidelity.co.uk/nmpa/)
- Most unused pension funds come into inheritance tax from 6 April 2027 (Finance Act 2026). — [Rathbones](https://www.rathbones.com/en-gb/wealth-management/knowledge-and-insight/autumn-budget-2026-what-it-means-for-you)
- Income tax thresholds are frozen until 5 April 2031. — [Rathbones](https://www.rathbones.com/en-gb/wealth-management/knowledge-and-insight/autumn-budget-2026-what-it-means-for-you)
- Annual allowance is £60,000 (or 100% of earnings if lower). After flexibly accessing a pension, the money purchase annual allowance is £10,000. — [GOV.UK Tax on your private pension: annual allowance](https://www.gov.uk/tax-on-your-private-pension/annual-allowance) (not fetched)
- Tax relief: the provider adds 20% basic-rate relief ("relief at source"). Higher and additional-rate taxpayers claim the rest. — [GOV.UK Tax on your private pension](https://www.gov.uk/tax-on-your-private-pension/pension-tax-relief) (not fetched)
- Choices at retirement: annuity, drawdown, cash lump sums, or a mix. Pension Wise (MoneyHelper) is free, impartial guidance for over-50s with a defined contribution pension. — [GOV.UK Pension Wise](https://www.gov.uk/pension-wise) (not fetched); [MoneyHelper Pension Wise](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/pensions-and-retirement/pension-wise) (not fetched)

### Inferences
- For beginners, the "tax relief = £80 becomes £100" example is the key hook. SIPP versus workplace pension is a secondary detail.

### Gaps
- Annual allowance, MPAA, tapered allowance and the relief mechanics were not re-verified for 2026/27 this session. No Budget change to them was reported in sources read.

## 4. ISAs (incl. 28 Oct 2026 Budget changes)

### Takeaway
**Confirmed:** the planned cut of the cash ISA limit to £12,000 (for under-65s from April 2027) was scrapped on 28 Oct 2026. The £20,000 overall limit stays and can all be cash. The planned anti-circumvention rules for stocks and shares ISAs were also dropped. The Lifetime ISA closes to new savers in April 2029, replaced by a First-Time Buyer ISA. A "starter" incentive for first-time stocks and shares ISA investors is being explored.

### Cited Findings
- The cash ISA limit stays at £20,000 for all ages. The Reeves plan for £12,000 from April 2027 for under-65s won't go ahead. — [MSE](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/news/2026/10/cash-isa-limit-cut-cancelled/); [Rathbones](https://www.rathbones.com/en-gb/wealth-management/knowledge-and-insight/autumn-budget-2026-what-it-means-for-you); [NatWest update 28 Oct 2026](https://www.natwest.com/savings/isa-overview/autumn-budget.html)
- Also scrapped: the planned 22% charge on interest from cash held in stocks and shares ISAs, and the ban on transfers from stocks and shares ISAs to cash ISAs for under-65s. — [MSE](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/news/2026/10/cash-isa-limit-cut-cancelled/)
- The government will "work with industry to explore a new ISA incentive for people investing in a stocks and shares ISA for the first time". No details yet. — [MSE](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/news/2026/10/cash-isa-limit-cut-cancelled/)
- Some outdated pages still show the £12,000 cut, e.g. a [SJP Facebook post](https://www.facebook.com/sjpwealth/posts/the-autumn-budget-at-a-glancefrom-cash-isas-and-a-potential-new-incentive-for-fi/1660016882801696/). Treat it as superseded.
- The official HMRC "Fiscal Events 2026 factsheets" page still lists "ISA reform, including anti-circumvention rules" but now holds only two other factsheets. — [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/fiscal-events-2026-factsheets)
- **Lifetime ISA:**
  - Open at 18 to 39. Save up to £4,000 a year (implied by the £1,000 maximum bonus). 25% bonus (max £1,000 a year) until 50. Property cap stays £450,000.
  - From 29 Oct 2026, withdrawing for a first home above £450,000 costs 20% (you lose only the bonus) instead of 25%.
  - Other non-qualifying withdrawals (not a first home and not at 60+) still cost 25%.
  - New LISAs can be opened until April 2029. Existing holders can keep paying in after that.
  - [MSE](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/news/2026/10/budget-lifetime-isa/)
- **First-Time Buyer ISA:** launches April 2029. Age 18+ with no upper age limit. £4,000 a year. 25% bonus on the final balance (including growth), maximum £20,000 lifetime bonus. Help to Buy ISAs can be transferred in. You can hold one alongside a LISA. — [MSE](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/news/2026/10/budget-lifetime-isa/); [Rathbones](https://www.rathbones.com/en-gb/wealth-management/knowledge-and-insight/autumn-budget-2026-what-it-means-for-you)
- Savings income tax rises a further 2 percentage points from 6 April 2027 (4 points in total with the 2025 Budget rise). This makes ISAs more valuable for cash savers. — [Rathbones](https://www.rathbones.com/en-gb/wealth-management/knowledge-and-insight/autumn-budget-2026-what-it-means-for-you)
- CGT stays at 18%/24%, and the annual exempt amount stays at £3,000. — [Rathbones](https://www.rathbones.com/en-gb/wealth-management/knowledge-and-insight/autumn-budget-2026-what-it-means-for-you)
- Junior ISA limit £9,000 a year. The child gets control at 16 and can withdraw at 18. — [GOV.UK Junior ISAs](https://www.gov.uk/junior-individual-savings-accounts) (not fetched)

### Inferences
- The LISA lesson needs a "rules are changing" banner. It's still open to 18 to 39 year olds now and closes to new savers April 2029. Its retirement use (withdraw penalty-free from 60) has no equivalent in the FTB ISA.
- Rathbones' wording that the LISA is "replaced" fits the MSE detail: existing LISAs continue, new ones stop in April 2029.

### Gaps
- No GOV.UK/HMRC primary Budget document on the ISA measures was fetched. All Budget ISA facts rest on MSE, Rathbones and NatWest. Check the Budget 2026 "Red Book" or HMRC policy papers before publishing.
- The Junior ISA limit wasn't re-verified for 2026/27.

## 5. Investing basics, protection and scams

### Takeaway
FSCS covers bank deposits up to **£120,000** per person per banking licence (since 1 Dec 2025, not £85,000). Investments are covered up to **£85,000** per person per firm, only if the firm fails, never for market falls. Check every firm on the FCA Register and Warning List (ScamSmart). Pension and investment fraud losses are large.

### Cited Findings
- Deposit protection rose from £85,000 to **£120,000** on 1 December 2025. It applies per eligible person, per authorised firm or banking licence. Temporary high balances are covered up to £1.4m. — [FSCS deposit limit](https://www.fscs.org.uk/what-we-cover/banks-building-societies-credit-unions/deposit-limit/); [PRA PS24/25](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/prudential-regulation/publication/2025/november/depositor-protection-policy-statement); [FSCS stakeholder page](https://www.fscs.org.uk/industry-resources/deposit-limit-change-stakeholder-materials/)
- **Correction to the brief:** "£85,000 deposits" is out of date. Use £120,000 for deposits and £85,000 for investments.
- Investments: up to £85,000 per eligible person, per firm (firm failures after 1 Apr 2019). — [FSCS investments](https://www.fscs.org.uk/what-we-cover/investments/)
- Investment fraud: UK victims lost £2.4m a day in 2025. Losses averaged £25,612 per person, often pension savings. — [City of London Police, Apr 2026](https://www.cityoflondon.police.uk/news/city-of-london/news/2026/april/uk-victims-lost-2.4-million-every-day-to-investment-fraud-in-2025---equivalent-to-1675-a-minute-city-of-london-police-reveal/)
- Pension fraud losses in 2024 were £17.5m, average about £34,000 per person (Action Fraud/NFIB). — [TPR pension scams](https://www.thepensionsregulator.gov.uk/en/pension-scams)
- The FCA ScamSmart campaign said the average victim lost £82,000, about "22 years of pension savings". This is an older campaign figure, so date it if used. — [FCA press release](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/press-releases/22-years-pension-savings-gone-24-hours)
- FCA Warning List: if you deal with an unauthorised firm "it's unlikely you'd get your money back". — [FCA Warning List](https://www.fca.org.uk/consumers/warning-list-unauthorised-firms)
- Check firms on the FCA Register. — [register.fca.org.uk](https://register.fca.org.uk/)
- Index funds and platform fees: no authoritative source was fetched this session. MoneyHelper and MSE beginner guides exist. — [MSE investing for beginners](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/savings/investment-beginners/) (not fetched)

### Inferences
- Lesson framing: FSCS protects you against a firm failing, not against bad investments. Spread cash over £120k across banking licences.

### Gaps
- Platform fee ranges and index fund cost data (e.g. FCA research on ongoing charges) were not researched.
- Current ScamSmart pension scam statistics newer than the press release weren't found.

## Suggested prioritised UK lesson list (for the report writer)
1. Your workplace pension: auto-enrolment, the free 3%, why not to opt out.
2. State Pension: 35 years, check your forecast, age 67, the triple lock changing in 2030.
3. ISAs: £20k allowance, cash vs stocks and shares, LISA now and the First-Time Buyer ISA in 2029.
4. Investing basics: index funds, fees, time horizon, risk.
5. Is my money safe? FSCS £120k/£85k, the FCA Register.
6. Scams: ScamSmart, pension scams, Warning List.
7. Pension tax relief, personal pensions/SIPPs, the annual allowance.
8. Finding lost pensions: Pension Tracing Service, dashboards from 2027/28.
9. Taking your pension: 57 from 2028, 25% tax-free, drawdown vs annuity, Pension Wise.
